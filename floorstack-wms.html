Portfolio-wide — what this is worth across all A/B SKUs

Count methods — matched to level

Once it's racked, "count the pallet" means something different at L1 than at L5. Three methods, three reach profiles, used together — and for L3-L5, both faster and safer than the old way: a person in a safety cage riding the forklift up to count by eye. Count is a module of WMS, not a separate product — open the full count control center →

GoPro on forklift

L3–L5
  • ✓ Camera rides the mast, captures each level as it passes
  • ✓ No manual scan — operator just lifts
  • ✓ No one riding a safety cage at height to count by eye
  • ✕ Ties up a forklift that could be picking

Drone

L1–L5
  • ✓ Whole rack face, all levels, one pass, seconds
  • ✓ Best for a fast whole-DC sweep
  • ✓ Zero people at height, zero cage on the forklift
  • ✕ Lower ID-read rate on partly blocked pallets
  • ✕ Needs the aisle clear of forklift traffic

The real count saving isn't walking — it's the cage

Below L3, count time is the story. Above it, staffing is: a caged cycle count needs the forklift operator plus a spotter riding the cage, both tied up for every high-bay session, every cycle. Automate the camera and that labor is gone, not just faster.
