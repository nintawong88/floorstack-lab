Approach

Most supply chain advice stops at the recommendation.

The report lands, the workshop finishes, and the operation goes back to what it was doing. The analysis wasn't wrong. Nobody stayed to build the thing that changes the behaviour.

My career was built inside operations, not around them. Siam Makro taught me retail distribution at scale. DHL Exel, Linfox, CEVA and Toll Global taught me contract logistics across the Asia-Pacific. Manufacturing taught me that the constraint is almost never the strategy.

So I work at both ends. I will sit in the board meeting and argue the network design. I will also open SAP Business ByDesign and rebuild the BOM structure, or write the software when the ERP genuinely cannot do what the operation needs.

That combination is unusual, and it is the entire proposition: strategy that gets implemented, because the person who designed it is still there when it goes live.
