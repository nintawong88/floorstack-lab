About

Why this page exists

I spent nearly three decades running distribution centers and procurement across Thailand: cold chain, FMCG, commercial refrigeration. I learned SAP eWM the hard way, on live floors, with real deadlines. AI is the same kind of tool: useful the moment you stop treating it as theory and point it at an actual bin, an actual PO, an actual stock count that's wrong.

This page is where I write down what actually works, what breaks, and what I'd do differently, as I build it, not after. For the consulting side of the work, see the main profile.
